# Script doctor and quality gate

Review as an independent reader before offering repairs. Give the concrete problem, location (scene/line/beat), audience consequence, smallest effective change, tradeoff, and severity: blocker / major / minor / optional. Preserve locked material until explicit permission to revise. When asked to write a revised version, return the complete revised script, version increment, and concise change log. Do not silently add historical facts, change personalities, dialogue, timing or a prior ending.

## Rubrica diagnóstica opcional, unificada na V3.1

Usar os critérios e a escala 0–5 de `../modules/quality-gates.md` somente quando uma nota ajudar a revisão, com evidência por eixo e cobertura. A antiga escala 0–4 fica preservada no ZIP V3 em legacy e não governa a V3.1. Nenhum total supera bloqueante ou substitui decisão humana. O padrão é problema, trecho, consequência, menor reparo e trade-off, sem nota decorativa. Não confundir diagnóstico técnico com excelência artística.

## Repair order

1. Canon/facts and central dramatic question.
2. Agency, stakes and causal links.
3. Setup/payoff, information asymmetry and ending.
4. Emotional credibility and dialogue.
5. Runtime, visual emphasis and polish.

Never optimize a local hook at the cost of the whole episode. For alternative drafts, compare axes and give examples; prefer the one with strongest narrative coherence, not the one with the most violence or edits.

# Reusable templates

## Episode pitch
Episode/version | Logline | Dramatic question | Protagonist desire | Obstacle | Choice | Cost | Emotional promise | Payoff | Ending | Factual constraints / fictional invention | Estimated duration | Risks.

## Beat sheet
| Beat ID | Estimated start/end | Event/choice | Cause from prior beat | What changes | Character and audience knowledge | Emotional purpose | Open question / payoff | Evidence / fiction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Screenplay
`EP XX — title — version — estimated runtime`  
`EXT./INT. LOCATION — TIME`  
Observable action in present tense.  
`CHARACTER`  
Dialogue.  
Repeat by scene. Separate notes, camera and optional suggestions from the screenplay unless the user explicitly requests an annotated shooting script.

## Doctor report
| Scene/beat | Problem | Evidence | Viewer impact | Severity | Proposed change | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Follow with applicable rubric axes, score and assumptions; identify approvals required, blockers and unanswered questions.

## Episode handoff
Episode/version/status; opening promise; payoff delivered; last approved event; unresolved hook; next possible entry points; timeline; character knowledge/state; location/props; facts and open questions; changes from previous version; approval status.
